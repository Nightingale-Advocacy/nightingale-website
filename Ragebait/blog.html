AI Meets the Pill: A Balance of Innovation, Patient Safety, and Regulation Capsule Endoscopy

Yashesvii Solleti, 9/26/2026

Swallowable cameras offer a sneak peek into the growing role of artificial intelligence in medicine as new innovations ever-evolve the field. Solleti explores how AI-assisted capsule endoscopy raises questions about patient safety, physician oversight, privacy, equitable access, and regulation.

The ACA’s Second Act

Noor Asim, 9/26/2026

Explore Asim's analysis of how the Affordable Care Act’s premium tax credits evolved, what the expiration of enhanced subsidies means for insurance affordability, and the policy choices that could shape the future of Marketplace coverage.

Psychological Reactance to COVID-19 Mandates and Its Legacy on Public Health

Gianna Lee, 9/26/2026

How does a perceived threat to personal freedom shape responses to public health policy? Lee analyzes the psychological reactance to COVID-19 mandates and its lasting implications for vaccine acceptance, institutional trust, and future health communication.

Mental Health Coverage: How Has Policy Impacted Mental Health Rates Compared to Physical Health?

Eishal Tashfeen, 9/26/2026

Mental health parity promises coverage on equal terms with physical health, but policy and funding decisions can complicate access to care. Tashfeen examines the gap between legal protections and the barriers patients face when seeking mental health treatment.

The Birth and Development of Medicaid

Aahee Kher, 9/26/2026

Trace Medicaid’s development from its creation in 1965 through decades of expanded eligibility and benefits. Kher examines how the program grew, the challenges it has faced, and why its history matters for healthcare advocacy today.

A Review of Reproductive Rights Under the Trump Administration

Crystal Gao, 04/07/2026

Gao's powerful prose guides the reader through a journey--a journey facing the brute reality of reproductive healthcare legislation transformed underneath the Trump Administration. As readers explore cuts to welfare programs, information erasure, and changed communities, take it as a call for action, a message for nationwide youth to march beyond the sidelines.

A Big Beautiful Mess: The Trump Administration's Early Attacks on Universities and Healthcare

Aditi Mudugere, 04/07/2026

Mudugere’s work highlights the ironically un-beautiful effects of legislation underneath the Trump Administration: education costs through the roof, rural healthcare in disarray, and an encroachment on free speech. By empowering us with information, she inspires students to reject complacency and stand against these unsightly attacks on American liberty.

Historical Undervaluation of Endometriosis

Dia Bangolae, 04/07/2026

Bangolae comprehensively breaks the silence regarding women’s health’s misrepresentation, underlining key gaps within the treatment and diagnosis of endometriosis. Millions of women suffer through underdiagnosed diseases, their pain regarded as myth and mystery. Bangolae hopes to shatter this oppressive system, empowering youth to speak up for their rights.
